About

A garden is a living work of art.

Founded by Reuben Wennink, WenninkWorks provides precision garden maintenance and landscape refinement. We work with the land — not against it — ensuring every cut, every planting, and every detail serves the long-term beauty of your outdoor space.

We operate all year round — across every season, from the first growth of spring through to winter care. Whatever the time of year, your garden is in expert hands.

Garden maintenance packages are available starting from a 2-hour minimum slot.

Services

What we do.

Precision Maintenance

Hedge trimming, lawn care, and borders kept to a meticulous standard.

Topiary

Sculpted shapes and formal hedging that give a garden its structure and character.

Plant Health Care

Nutrient management, pest control, and soil optimization for long-term vitality.

Vegetable Sowing

Sowing and growing vegetables for your kitchen — from seed to harvest, we handle the full cycle.

Irrigation Systems

Installation of water irrigation systems to keep your landscape healthy with minimal effort.

Seasonal Planting

Considered planting schemes that bring colour and structure throughout the year.

Jet Washing

Professional jet washing of driveways and hard surfaces, restoring them to a clean, fresh finish.

Aquatic Pond Plants

Selection, planting, and care of aquatic plants to create a thriving, balanced pond environment.

Plastic Clearance

Removal of plastic debris and waste from garden spaces to protect soil health and reduce microplastic contamination.

Custom-designed and 3D printed indoor flower vases — a unique, handcrafted product to bring natural beauty inside your home.
