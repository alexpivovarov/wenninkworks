Mastering the Art of Nature

At WenninkWorks, we believe that a garden is more than just an outdoor space—it is a living, breathing work of art that requires precision, care, and a deep understanding of horticulture. Founded by Reuben Wennink, our team is dedicated to providing high-end garden maintenance and landscape refinement.

We work with nature, ensuring that every trim, every planting, and every lighting placement enhances the beauty and longevity of your estate for the future.

What We Do

We offer a focused range of services to keep your landscape looking its best, season after season.

  • Precision Maintenance Hedge trimming, topiary, and lawn care — every line sharp, every plant thriving.
  • Plant Health Care Nutrient management, pest control, and soil optimization for long-term vitality.
  • Seasonal Planting Thoughtful planting schemes that bring colour and structure throughout the year.
